# Dead Ends — Failed Approaches

### [Short description]
- **Tried**: [what was attempted]
- **Failed because**: [specific reason]
- **Date**: [when]
- **Do not retry**: [yes/no — yes means permanently dead, no means might work with changes]
- **Alternative**: [what worked instead, if known]
